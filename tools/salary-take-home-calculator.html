How the salary take-home calculator works

Take-home pay is the amount left from gross salary after estimated tax and other deductions. This calculator lets you enter your own tax percentage rather than assuming a universal tax table, because payroll rules differ by country and tax year.

How to use it

Enter your gross annual salary, estimated tax rate and other annual deductions. If your compensation includes a separately valued benefit, add it to the benefit field to compare total compensation with cash take-home pay.

Why your actual paycheck can differ

Payroll can include progressive tax bands, social contributions, insurance, retirement contributions, allowances and taxable benefits. Check your employer payroll statement or official tax authority for the exact amount.

Frequently asked questions

Is this calculator an official tax calculator?

No. It is an estimation tool designed for planning and comparison. It does not replace professional tax advice or an official government calculator.

Can I use it for any country?

Yes, as a general estimator if you know the relevant effective tax rate and deductions. For country-specific results, use the applicable official tax rules and tax year.
