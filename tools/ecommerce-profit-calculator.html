Why calculate e-commerce profit?

Revenue alone does not show whether a product is profitable. Marketplace commissions, payment processing, fulfillment and advertising can materially reduce the amount left from each sale.

What this calculator assumes

The calculation treats percentage fees as a percentage of the selling price and adds the fixed costs you enter. Refunds, returns, storage and taxes may require additional assumptions.
