What this ATS checker measures

This browser-based checker extracts repeated, meaningful words from the job description and compares them with your resume text. It is an educational matching tool, not a replica of a proprietary ATS.

Improve your resume match

Use relevant skills and experience that you genuinely have. Do not add keywords that are false or misleading. Keep formatting simple and readable.

Privacy

Text is processed in your browser by this page. Avoid pasting sensitive personal information when testing.
