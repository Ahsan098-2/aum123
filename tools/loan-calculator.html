How EMI Is Calculated

EMI is calculated using the loan amount, the monthly interest rate, and the number of months in your tenure, so that equal payments fully repay both principal and interest by the end of the loan. This loan emi calculator applies that formula instantly for any loan amount or rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is EMI and how is it calculated?

EMI (Equated Monthly Installment) is calculated from your loan amount, monthly interest rate, and tenure, so the loan is fully repaid in equal monthly payments that cover both principal and interest.

Is this loan calculator free for personal, car and home loans?

Yes, this loan calculator is completely free and works for any loan type — personal, car, or home — as long as you know the loan amount, interest rate, and tenure.

How can I reduce my total loan interest?

You can lower total interest by choosing a shorter tenure, making extra or prepayments toward the principal, or comparing interest rates across lenders before taking the loan.
