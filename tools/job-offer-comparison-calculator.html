How to compare job offers

Headline salary is only one part of compensation. Bonuses, employer-paid benefits and recurring commuting costs can change the practical value of an offer. This calculator is a simple comparison framework, not a tax calculation.

What is not included?

Taxes, equity, paid time off, insurance details and career growth are not automatically valued. Add your own estimates when they can be measured reliably.
