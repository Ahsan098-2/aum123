What is a break-even point?

The break-even point is where total contribution covers fixed costs, leaving zero operating profit under the assumptions entered.

Formula

Break-even units = fixed costs ÷ (selling price − variable cost). If variable cost is equal to or greater than selling price, the business cannot reach break-even using these assumptions.
