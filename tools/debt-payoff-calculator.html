How debt payoff is calculated

For a fixed monthly payment, each payment first covers accrued interest and the remainder reduces principal. The calculator repeats that process until the balance reaches zero.

What if my payment is too low?

If the monthly payment does not exceed the first month's interest, the debt cannot be paid off under the fixed-payment assumption. Increase the payment or check the lender's terms.

Use this as a planning estimate

Fees, variable rates, missed payments and lender-specific interest calculations can change the real result. Confirm your balance and terms with your lender before making financial decisions.

FAQ

Does this include fees?

No. Add recurring fees to your own planning or use the lender's statement for an exact payoff quote.
