Frequently asked questions

Good to know before you order

Can I use the barcode generator for free?

Yes. You can create your own barcode with the free Daily Toolkit barcode generator. The paid service is optional help with formatting and label layout.

Will a generated barcode automatically be approved for retail products?

No. A barcode image is not the same as an officially assigned or registered retail product identifier. Retailers and marketplaces may require GS1-issued identifiers or their own rules. Check the requirements of your retailer or marketplace before ordering labels.

Do you print and ship physical labels?

Not as part of these digital packages. Printing and delivery are excluded unless separately discussed and confirmed.

Which file format will I receive?

We will confirm the suitable format before work begins. Depending on the task, this may be PDF, PNG or SVG. Not every format is suitable for every printing workflow.

How long will the work take?

Delivery timing depends on the scope and the quality of the information you provide. We will confirm an estimated delivery time before starting.

Can you make labels in my exact size?

Share the label width and height, the sheet or sticker-paper layout if applicable, and a photo or link to the label stock. We will confirm whether it can be supported before accepting the job.

When do I pay?

We will confirm the agreed scope, price, payment method and delivery expectations before work begins. Do not send payment until those details are clear.
